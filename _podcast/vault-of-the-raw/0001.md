Allow me to tell you a little story. A [thing]({% link _podcast/vault-of-the-raw/0002.md %}) happened. I didn't expect it to happen, and I wasn't even sure it was going to happen until right before it did. The sound of a gong seemed to free me, and words just came out despite my initial plan. Getting that out was cathartic, and I was able to re-record the episode staying true to the concept. In 40 episodes, this was the first retake *ever*. Pretty impressive if I do say so myself.

It was already too late. The seed of an idea was planted. A bit later in the day I went for a pleasant walk, and decided to record some rambling, which wound up being the watering and care a seed needs to grow into something more substantial. A small stem sprouted, a mandala in podcast form. Vault of the Raw was born, and now here it is.
