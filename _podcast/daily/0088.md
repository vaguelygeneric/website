The controversy surrounding a word, but only in the US it seems. Why I don't use it, and why I don't have trouble reading it.

**niggardly** */nĭg′ərd-lē/*

adjective

* Grudging and petty in giving or spending.
* Meanly small; scanty or meager. "left the waiter a niggardly tip."
* Meanly covetous or avaricious in dealing with others; stingy; niggard.
* Withholding for the sake of meanness; stingy, miserly.
* Petty or reluctant in giving or spending.

adverb

* In a parsimonious way; sparingly, stingily.
