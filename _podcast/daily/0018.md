**label** */lā′bəl/*

1. a piece of paper, cloth, or similar material that is attached to something to identify or describe it
2. a word or phrase that describes or identifies something or someone
3. a company that produces musical recordings
4. a name shown on clothes that indicates the store, company, or person who sold, produced, or designed the clothes

verb

1. to put a word or name on something to describe or identify it : to attach a label to (something)
2. to name or describe (someone or something) in a specified way : to give a label to (someone or something)
