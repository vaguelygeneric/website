Just a quick one today. A Darkness Hovers

<blockquote>
  <div class="blockquote-header">
    <h3>A Darkness Hovers</h3>
    <p class="blockquote-author">John Lawless</p>
  </div>
  <div class="blockquote-content">
<p>
A darkness hovers
A silence whispers
A shadow threatens
Every movement
A choreographed dance routine

Places, places everyone
The new ABC’s
Aggravate
Belittle
Confuse

The new math
One is never too many
And a thousand
Is just the beginning

The new “religion”
A well funded
Burt and Ernie
Puppet show

Everyone is watching everyone
Hovering in a darkness
Whispering silently
PLACES….PLACES
ACTION
TAKE(WHAT YOU CAN)
</p>
  </div>
