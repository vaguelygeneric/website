Friends With Ghosts
{% poem title="Friends With Ghosts" author=" tripp j crouse" source_title="Friends With Ghosts By tripp j crouse" source_url="https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poetrymagazine/poems/1815013/friends-with-ghosts" %}
Niizh manidoowag
is the Ojibwemowin phrase
from which the term
“Two Spirit” is derived.

I once asked my mom
how to pronounce niizh manidoowag. 
She said, “That means ‘friends with ghosts.’
Why would you be friends with ghosts?”

I said that oftentimes
they are more reliable
than people.

This one time I asked my mom
how to say niizh manidoowag.
She said, “That means ‘friends with ghosts.’ 
Why would you be friends with ghosts?”

Because they have the best jokes, I quip.

A while back, I asked my mom
how to say niizh manidoowag.
She said, “That means ‘friends with ghosts.’ 
Why would you be friends with ghosts?”

And I tell her
who wouldn’t want
             to divorce themselves
from the living, the present
             and be lost to time
forever.

But I wonder
when my mom passes on,
would she want
             to be friends
                          with
                           me. 

{% endpoem %}
